### Charts the tool draws (`charts/`; built 2026-10-21)

The weekly Equities section draws two charts itself, NASI P/E and NASI dividend yield, from the uploaded equities workbook's 'NASI-PE & Dividend Yield' sheet. Nothing else is drawn: every other chart stays `[Chart to add: <caption>]`. A chart's numbers are the analysts' typed workbook values, labelled "from the analyst workbook, not verified"; no model touches them, and no chart XML is read at run time.

- **Reading** (`charts/specs.py`, `charts/series.py`): a series is found by sheet name and header text, never a row number, and each value is paired with the date in its own row. A value cell that is empty or text, a value with no date, a repeated date, or a pair of rows whose dates are out of order is listed by cell and left out (both rows of a pair, because the workbook does not say which is wrong); nothing is repaired. A missing sheet or header, a number format that contradicts the unit, too few points, a stale newest point and any point after the ceiling stop the chart (`drawn` is False). The average is the exact mean of every point from `AVERAGE_FROM` (2009-01-01), rounded half up once, compared with the workbook's own AVERAGE cell at display precision; the picture starts on the first of the week-ending date's month, `NASI_WINDOW_YEARS` (7) years earlier (`window_start` in `charts/series.py`; Ryan, 3 Nov 2026: the window follows the report month and is fixed within it, and the October 2026 published charts start at Oct-19), and runs to the newest point; a series that starts later is shown from its first point. The average is not limited to the window. `Sheet.find_all` treats an empty row range as the whole sheet (a latent trap in `weekly/workbook.py`, on the backlog); the reader never asks for one.
- **Drawing** (`charts/draw.py`): matplotlib 3.11.2 (pinned; it needs Python 3.11), the `Figure` and Agg canvas directly and never pyplot, 6.0 x 3.0 in at 200 dpi. The look is the published one, from `common/report_theme.py` (2026-11-04): "Cytonn Report: <name>" centred at the top, bold, with a drawn underline; a `#006C5B` line 2.25 pt with no fill and no gridlines; the y axis from 0 by steps of 2 (P/E to 19, the dividend yield to the next multiple of 2 above the data); a red dashed average line 1.5 pt, its "Average = ..." label placed where the series is furthest from the line across the label's width; tick, average and end labels in bold black 9 pt. The font is Calibri, else Carlito, else the bundled DejaVu Sans with one warning (`chart_font()`); the Dockerfile installs `fonts-crosextra-carlito` (added 2026-11-04, the image not yet rebuilt). The image is not built or run in Docker since matplotlib was added; it runs as the user `cytonn` with a home folder, so its font cache (`~/.cache/matplotlib`) is writable and `MPLCONFIGDIR` is not set.
- **The block and its review** (`charts/block.py`): metadata only, in one of three states. `drawn`: five review items (the last point against the figure the draft quotes, the average against the AVERAGE cell and the quoted average, freshness, the rows left out, and "from the analyst workbook, not verified"). `not_drawn` (a workbook was uploaded but cannot give the chart): flagged items that hold the section up. `no_workbook`: information with no review item, so it never holds up approval. The flag kinds `stale` and `series_defect` follow the link kinds.
- **Files** (`charts/store.py`): `<data dir>/charts/<run_id>/<chart_id>.png` and `.json`, created exclusively when the review is first saved, so a file is never replaced and drafting again makes a new run and a new folder. The block holds both SHA-256s. `GET /api/reviews/{run_id}/charts/{chart_id}.png` and `.../series` check the hash on every read (409 when the file changed, 404 when it is missing, the chart unknown or not drawn); `chart_id` is checked against `SPECS` before any path is built. The Word export reads the picture through the same check.
- **The team's own chart:** `PUT /api/reviews/{run_id}/charts/{chart_id}/team-chart` accepts every item of the chart with a fixed note, and the choice **is** that state. A saved review's draft content is written once while its items are rewritten on every save, so the choice is stored as accepted items; it is saved with the review, ends if any one of those items is changed, and is refused once the review is decided or for a chart nothing was read for. "Charts to add by hand" lists a chart that was not drawn or was set aside, and never one that was drawn and is in use.
- **The screen** (`ChartCard.vue`): the picture in a white frame in both themes, the facts it rests on, the rows left out listed by cell, "Show points" (a collapsed table read from the stored series), "Open full size" and the "Use the team's own chart" button. The panel rule holds for it: its notices are in the draft's column and its checks are items in the Review items panel, which never folds.
- **Word export** (`weekly/export.py`, `_chart`): a drawn chart in use is the stored picture 6.0 in wide at the `chart_after` position, the caption as its alt text, and its own paragraph "From the analyst workbook, not verified." The team's own chart or no workbook leaves the placeholder alone. A chart not drawn that the analyst accepted as missing leaves the placeholder and a red `[Chart not drawn: <reason>]`; a drawn chart whose file is changed or missing leaves the placeholder and a red `[Chart not drawn: the stored picture could not be verified]`. A dev-mode export is watermarked as before and embeds the charts the same way.
- **A chart image a person uploads** (`weekly/chart_uploads.py`, stored per week with `charts.json` beside the images) wins over a drawn chart or a placeholder in the Word export. Its slot takes a lead-in printed above the image and, since 2026-10-30, an optional source line (`PUT .../charts/{key}/source`): one line, at most 120 characters after trimming, stored as typed and shown as plain text. It prints as "Source: <text>" under the image, on the slot in the inputs panel and in the export (`_source_line`, so `small_line`). Empty means nothing prints: no source is ever filled in, and neither the text nor the image goes to a model. Deleting the image removes its source and keeps its lead-in.
- **A designed difference between the form and the workbook:** typing a workbook's figures on the analyst form gives the same sections as uploading it, except for these charts, which need the workbook's history that the form does not hold. Without a workbook each is `no_workbook`.
- **Demo and tests:** `e2e/chart_demo.py` stores invented workbooks for four Fridays (the chart tests, T78 to T86; `E2E_SHOTS=<folder>` writes screenshots); `tests/chart_helpers.py` builds invented NASI sheets with the real sheet name and layout, and `tests/test_charts_replay_real.py` replays the readers on the real workbook (committed without any data, asserts relations only, skips without the sample folder). The rest of the second group of charts (T-bill yields and subscription, interbank, import cover, money market, listed-bank yield, turnover and foreign flows, the 91-day yield growth) waits for the team to confirm each published chart's block and definition.
