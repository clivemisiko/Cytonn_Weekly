### Equities (`equities/weekly.py`)

The real weekly prints **no indices table and no gainers or losers table**, so none is printed (afx stays as the OCR cross-check; `equities/review_run.py`'s afx-table builder is no longer in the registry and serves only as a stand-in in older tests). Market Performance: the four indices' week, ordered by the size of the move, and year to date (NASI, NSE 20 and NSE 25 from KCB's weekly report; NSE 10 and the Banking index by OCR for the two Fridays; year opens by OCR of the year's first trading day's list); the large-cap movers (the investments team's rule, Ryan, 4 Nov 2026: up to three rises and up to three falls among the twelve large caps on the workbook's 'Gainers & Losers' sheet, each worked out from the prices in the columns dated this Friday and the Friday before, found by their exact date in row 2, with KCB's week-on-week change as the second reading; a change of exactly zero is neither, a tie keeps the sheet's row order; without a workbook, the biggest movers among the typed large-cap list by KCB's changes, flagged "check the names"); the Banking index, followed by the same rule over the banks among the twelve (`BANKS` in `equities/weekly.py`: EQTY, KCB, COOP, ABSA, SCBK, DTBK, NCBA, STANBIC, to be confirmed with Ryan), naming DTBK as DTB-K, and no stocks without a workbook; turnover in USD (each day's KES turnover over that day's CBK rate: KCB's daily report, else the bulletin's Table 6, else the price list by OCR; the bulletin covers Friday to Thursday, so with it only the Friday needs KCB's daily report); foreign investors' net position in USD (KCB's weekly report lists each weekday) with the streak and year-to-date totals from the workbook; the P/E against its average, the dividend yield against its average in percentage points, and the PEG (the P/E over a fixed 8). Then the Universe of Coverage table: w/w against last Friday, m/m against the previous month's last close, YTD against the 2 January close, dividend yield DPS over price, upside dividend yield plus target over price less one, P/TBV price over tangible book per share; recommendation Buy from 20%, Accumulate from 10%, Hold from 5%, Lighten from 0.1%, Sell at or below 0, and **nothing between 0% and 0.1%, which is flagged, not filled**; sorted by upside. Then the highlights and the outlook carried forward.

**Listed banks and insurers (2026-11-12; `equities/companies.py`, `equities/companies.json`, `CompaniesList.vue`).** A part of the weekly Equities, after the highlights and before the closing outlook, on 15 companies: banks Absa Bank Kenya, BK Group, Diamond Trust Bank Kenya, Equity Group Holdings, HF Group, I&M Group, KCB Group, NCBA Group, Stanbic Holdings, Standard Chartered Bank Kenya, Co-operative Bank of Kenya; insurers Britam, CIC Insurance Group, Jubilee Holdings, Sanlam Allianz Holdings (Kenya).
- **Config:** `companies.json` (name, type bank or insurer, domain, optional `ir` address; the `ir` address is shown, never fetched). Also optional: `scope` (a path prefix limiting the company to its Kenyan pages), `hosts` (extra hostnames of the company), `start` (full addresses where reading its site begins; none means its home page) and `kenya_only` (false for BK Group, so no country-code filter). A domain of "to confirm" (or anything that is not a hostname) means the website is not set. All 15 have a domain (2026-11-13); a start page that could not be confirmed is left empty, never guessed (Equity Group, I&M and Stanbic answered with a bot wall or 403; NCBA's /investor-reports answered 404). The file is read on every draft, but the known-sites label list is built at import, so restart the server after an edit. Banks come first, then insurers, each by name (no market size is available for all of them). A domain that is set is added to `sources.APPROVED_SITES` (a label only). `GET /api/equities/companies` serves the list with each company's status, and the Equities start screen shows it.
- **Sourcing:** each company with a domain is a source piece (`Piece.site`, `Piece.only_domains`). The gather reads the company's own website first (`common/site_read.py`, four companies at a time, 0.5 s between requests to one site): robots.txt of each host is read and a disallowed path is never fetched (a disallowed start page makes the site unreadable); up to 3 start pages are read (the home page and up to two investor, results or news pages from it when none is set); links are kept only when `Company.owns` accepts them (host is the domain, a subdomain on the dot boundary or one of `hosts`; path inside `scope`; first path segment not another country's code unless `kenya_only` is false) and the link looks like news or results; a link dated outside the report week is dropped, an undated one is fetched and dated by its page (`fetch_page(window=...)`, refused outside the week); up to 3 pages are saved through the usual guarded fetch and source store. When the site cannot be read or gives no dated page, the old search (1 per company, restricted to the domain and hosts, results filtered by `owns`, up to 2 pages) is the fallback. The route used (own website read, search fallback, nothing found) and why is kept with the look (`looks.json`) and shown in the part's notes. The gather's time limit is per section (`gather.SECTION_SECONDS`: Equities 420 s, others 150 s; `CYTONN_SOURCES_GATHER_SECONDS` overrides both). No robots.txt blocked a start page when checked on 2026-11-13. The Sources panel lists each company as a piece.
- **Drafting:** only a company with saved pages is drafted, one Bedrock narrative piece each (`FACTS` in its brief: only what its own pages say, a figure only with its sentence, no comparison, nothing from memory), with the usual word-for-word quote check; no pages, no model call. A first block (`company_notes`, a figure-less computed block) holds the part's heading and notes: "Not searched: website not set: ...", "Nothing this week: ..." and "Already shown in another part: <company> (in <part>)". A company whose claims cite only pages already cited by an earlier highlight (corporate actions, earnings, banking or regulation) is shown once, there (same address, not same event). The part is left out of the section summary.
