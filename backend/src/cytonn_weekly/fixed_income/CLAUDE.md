### Net domestic borrowing log (`fixed_income/net_borrowing.py`; built 2026-10-12)

The closing sentence's net borrowing is no longer read from the pasted or workbook log: it is built from CBK's own result files, so without a workbook and without a typed log the part is complete from CBK alone plus the typed full-year target.

- **What is read.** `cbk_auctions.parse_tbill_results` also returns `accepted_kes_m` and `redemptions_kes_m` (section A's "Total Amount Accepted" and "Rollover / Redemptions", per tenor and `total`) and `next_week_redemptions_kes_m` with `next_value_date` (section C). `parse_tbond_results` also returns `accepted_kes_m`, the sum of the per-issue cells (the printed total is rounded to a whole million: 17-08-2026 prints 312,027 for 312,027.34), and `redemptions_kes_m` ("-" is 0.0; None when the results state none, as a switch's and a tap sale's do not). **"New Borrowing/Net Repayment" is printed unsigned and is never read.** `fetch_period_tbills(start, end)` is the counterpart of `fetch_period_tbonds`: each file by the value date in its name.
- **The rule.** Net = accepted minus redemptions, release by release, from the fiscal year's first day to the latest counted release settling by the end of the week's value-date window; that release's value date is the as-at date of the pro-rating (days from the start over 364, times the target), shown in the pro-rated figure's note. A T-bill auction's accepted amount is the sum of its three tenors (CBK's printed total differs by 0.01 for three July 2026 auctions; one warning line says so). Worded "is at X% of", never "ahead of".
- **A switch auction never counts** (the investments team's rule, settled 2026-10-12): a switch is not new debt. Nothing on the form or in the code can make one count: there is no tick-box and no override amount. Every switch found is listed for information only, as a note on the `borrowing` part and on the log ("Switch auctions found, excluded by rule: <dates and CBK amounts>"), never as a review item or a flag. Its row in the log shows CBK's accepted amount, "Counted: No" and no net.
- **What the review shows.** The `borrowing` computed block (net, pro-rated target, share; the net is clean when every input is a figure CBK printed) and a `borrowing_log` table, one row per release (value date, auction, accepted, redemptions, net, counted, running net), `export: False`, so it is on the review screen and not in the Word file. Every row's sum, net and running net are worked out again by the table check (`_derived`).
- **Second readings, on the rows.** The previous T-bill release's section C against this release's redemptions, and an uploaded workbook's log row by row (paired by value date, the same accepted amount first). A difference is "sources disagree" on that row. A workbook row no CBK release matches, and a counted CBK release the workbook lacks, are warning lines.
- **A workbook log that counts a switch** is flagged on that switch's row: "workbook log counts a switch the rule excludes (<date>, <amount> mn)", whatever amount it holds. It is the workbook against the rule, never read as a CBK error, so the workbook's amount is not a second reading of CBK's (the row carries its own `_flags`, which `review/checks.check_table_block` turns into a "sources disagree" flag with that wording). When the workbook's own log, to the same value date, adds to a different net, one warning line sets old beside new and says what the difference is made of (`Log.workbook_difference`); the figure from CBK's results is the one printed, and nothing is forced to match.
- **A bond redemption on no result file is typed** (built 2026-10-12). CBK prints a bond redemption only in the results of a bond auction settling that day, so a bond maturing on a day with no bond auction is on no file (first case: IFB1/2014/12, 12 October 2026). The form's "Other bond redemptions" (`borrowing_redemptions`: value date, bond, amount in Kshs mn; any day of the fiscal year, amount positive, a bond listed once a day; carried forward and used at once) gives one log row per redemption in the window, kind "Bond redemption (typed, not on a release)", subtracted on its value date as analyst input: the row is not auto-verified and so is the net figure. The rows never pair with a workbook's log and are not added to a typed auction log or the workbook fallback (a warning line says so).
- **A maturity nobody accounted for is asked about, never filled in** (`fixed_income/bond_issues.py`). The "Issues of Treasury Bonds" table is in the treasury-bonds listing page the bond results are already fetched from, so it costs no request; it is read by its headings, summed per ISIN and maturity date, and kept as `bond_issues.json` beside the result files with the table's sha256 (the kept reading is the fallback, with a warning). A maturity date in the log's window with no bond result of that day stating redemptions and no typed row for that day is a log row "Bond maturity (no redemption found)", flagged (kind missing) "bond maturity on <date> (<bond>): no redemption on any release and none typed. CBK's table shows <face> mn, the face value at issue, which is only a floor; type the amount outstanding at maturity, as shown on the NSE Implied Yields sheet, under "Other bond redemptions""; it adds nothing to the net, and the net figure carries the same flag. **The table is incomplete** (333 rows on 2026-10-12, issue dates stop at 6 April 2026), so its totals are a floor: FXD1/2016/10 adds to 103,380.70 where the 17 August results print 118,137.00 of redemptions. **`KNOWN_WRONG_ROWS`** is the short, documented ignore list: IFB1/2023/007's row maturing 15/07/2026 is wrong (no fall in the bond stock that week) and never raises the question; nothing else is hidden.
- **Advisory stock check** (`net_borrowing.stock_check_block`, table `bond_stock_check`, `export: False`). The bulletin's "Government Domestic Debt" table (Table 7 in the 2 October 2026 issue; found by title, `Bulletin.bond_stock`) prints the outstanding bond stock at eight dates, the last two consecutive Fridays. For each such pair inside the log's window: the stock's change less the net of the log's bond rows (typed redemptions included) settling after the first Friday and by the second. A residual below `STOCK_RESIDUAL_FLOOR_BN` (-1.0 bn) is flagged "possible unprinted bond redemption"; a week with a switch or an unread result is left out and named in a note. Only this week's bulletin is read, so it is one interval a draft (18 to 25 September 2026: 53.95 less 50.18, residual +3.77), a week behind the report week.
- **Left out, and said so (2026-11-17):** `tbonds_blocks` adds a warning line for each tap sale or switch result it does not print (only primary auctions are) and for each CBK result file `fetch_period_tbonds` skipped (`skipped_files`: a name with no "DATED dd-mm-yyyy" among the listing's newest ten, or a value date within 7 days of the window); what is included is unchanged.
- **Never passed over:** a release that cannot be read makes the CBK log unusable (the fallback, or a named unavailable part); a gap of more than 9 days between T-bill value dates, and a tap sale that states no redemptions, flag the net figure.
- **The typed auction log is an override and the fallback.** Typed and confirmed, it is used instead of CBK's results, as analyst input, with CBK's net as the figure's second reading and a warning line. When CBK's results cannot be fetched or read, the typed log, else the uploaded workbook's, is used as before.
- **The files are kept and re-checked** (`ResultsCache`): `<CYTONN_DATA_DIR>/inputs/cbk_results/` holds each downloaded PDF, and `index.json` (sha256, size, CBK's size and modification time). The first run of a fiscal year downloads every file since 1 July; later runs download only what is new. Every run checks each kept file against its hash and, with one HEAD request, against CBK's current size and modification time; a file CBK replaced is downloaded again, the earlier copy is kept as `<name>.replaced-<sha8>.pdf`, and the change is a line in the section's warnings. Revisions happen: CBK restated the 26 August 2026 switch results after release (22,510.93 mn accepted in the original, 21,816.46 mn in the "... Final.pdf" it serves now). One second between downloads, half a second between checks.
- **On the form** (`weekly/analyst.py`): the full-year target (`borrowing_target`, from the Exchequer releases; carried forward and used at once, and typed again only when the National Treasury issues revised estimates) and `fiscal_year_start` (a date within the year before the week, 1 July when empty; a typed one is named in the pro-rated figure's note; it carries forward and is used at once, within the fiscal year). No switch table: a `borrowing_switches` table saved by an earlier build is ignored when the form is read, and saving one is refused.
- **Old against new, verified 2026-10-12 on CBK's 24 results from 1 July to 7 October 2026** (target 919.36 bn; not forced to match):

### Fixed Income (`fixed_income/weekly.py`)

In the issue's order: T-bills (subscription overall and by tenor against the previous week, acceptance, each tenor's yield and its change in bps: the yield is CBK's weighted average rate of accepted bids; from the bulletin's Table 4, cross-checked with the workbook); T-bonds (the week's primary results from CBK's results PDFs, cross-checked with the bulletin's Table 5) and **the analysts' recommended bidding range, their own text or an empty part, never a range the tool made up** (the NSE curve's yield at each auctioned bond's remaining tenor is shown as context only); Money Market Performance (3-month bank placements, the 91-day and 364-day yields, the Cytonn fund, the Top 5 funds' average, which is **the average of this week's five highest rates in the table**, and the ranked fund table); Liquidity (volumes from the bulletin's Table 3; the rate change from the workbook's daily rates, to two decimals of a basis point); Kenya Eurobonds (the biggest mover, or, when the five issues moved both ways, the biggest riser and the biggest faller ("led by X up Y bps, while Z fell by W bps", figure keys `riser_*` and `faller_*`; one bond only, keys `biggest_*`, when all moved one way), and the table: five issues, the 2025 11-year left out; days the bulletin prints come from the bulletin with the workbook as second source, the 2 January and month-start rows from the workbook; Amount Issued and Years to Maturity worked out from the workbook's issue list, to the table's last day; changes in percentage points); Kenya Shilling (Friday to Friday and year to date from CBK's rates; remittances; reserves from the bulletin's Table 2; the pressure bullets carried forward); the weekly highlights (cited narrative, as every section's); the closing outlook carried forward **without its own borrowing sentence** (the borrowing sentence stays its own block with its figures for the checker, marked `continues` the outlook's id, and the Word export prints it in the outlook's paragraph under one heading, the red carried note staying until the paragraph is edited; 2026-11-17), and the net domestic borrowing sentence worked out from CBK's auction results (see **Net domestic borrowing log**): net borrowing over the target pro-rated by days from the fiscal year's start to the latest auction's value date over a 364-day year, worded "is at X% of its pro-rated ... target" and never "ahead".
